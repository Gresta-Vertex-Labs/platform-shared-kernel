Phase 2 of WO-087 for this domain: every applicable rule of root "Observability, exceptions and error codes". The applicable set, rule by rule (the rest do not apply here and the decision is recorded so the next audit does not reopen it):

- **L1/L2.** Each of the six packages gets one `{Package}LoggingEventIds` and one `{Package}Log` under `Diagnostics/`, and its own 100-wide sub-block of 14000-14999 (D-01). Today Core's three ids sit inside WebApi's range, and the `Log` classes are nested privately in the handlers.
- **L3-L8.** All 24 `[LoggerMessage]` statements rewritten to the template rule, free text out, the exception passed on every caught-exception path, optional loggers made required, categories checked.

Tag-key constants (T4) come from `WellKnownTagKeys`; a governance run shows no raw tag literal here. Adding an error counter would duplicate the framework's `http.server.request.duration` with `error.type`.
- **E1-E4.** The domain defines no exception type; its messages and rethrows are swept (D-07).
- **C1-C3.** Codes already read `{domain}.{snake_reason}`; the rename follows from 01 (D-08).
- **One mapping (the entry's specific).** HTTP status, GraphQL `status`/`type` and the SignalR/gRPC server-category decisions derive from one table each with a consistency test, and no third copy remains (D-02).
- **The edge-mapper conformance matrix** that eleven other domains routed here (D-10).
